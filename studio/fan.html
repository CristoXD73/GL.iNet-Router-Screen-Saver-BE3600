The sound

The speed curve is real: spin-up, coast-down and dead time were measured on a BE3600 with its own tachometer, and loudness follows the fan law, 50 log₁₀ of speed. The timbre is a model, not a recording — nobody has put a microphone in the case. Tune the three sound sliders until the preview matches what you hear from the router, then design against it.
